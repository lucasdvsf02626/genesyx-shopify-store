Cookie Policy

Last updated: 19 March 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Genesyx Ltd uses cookies and similar technologies on www.genesyx.co.uk.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, remember preferences, improve performance and, in some cases, support analytics or marketing.

2. Do we use cookies?

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies provided by the website platform or other tools connected to the site.

At present, as a simple holding page, we intend to keep cookie use to a minimum. However, depending on how the site is configured, cookies may still be set by:

  • Shopify
  • embedded content or third-party integrations added later
  • analytics tools, if enabled in future

3. Types of cookies

Strictly necessary cookies
These are essential for the website to function properly. They may be used for security, network management or basic functionality. Under UK rules, these cookies do not usually require consent, but users still need to be told about them.

Analytics or performance cookies
These help website owners understand how visitors use a site. These are not usually strictly necessary, so consent is generally required before they are set.

Functionality cookies
These remember choices a user makes, such as preferences, to improve their experience. These may require consent unless they are genuinely essential.

Marketing or advertising cookies
These are used to track browsing behaviour and deliver targeted advertising. These require prior consent in the UK.

4. Cookies currently in use

Our current site is a simple holding page collecting email sign-ups only. Our current position is:

  • use only strictly necessary cookies unless and until we add analytics or advertising tools
  • do not enable non-essential cookies without a compliant cookie banner/consent mechanism
  • update this policy once the website expands

5. Consent

If our website uses only strictly necessary cookies, we may not need a full opt-in banner for those cookies alone. However, if we use any analytics, functionality or marketing cookies that are not strictly necessary, UK guidance says we should:

  • show a clear cookie banner when users first visit the site
  • allow users to accept or reject non-essential cookies
  • avoid setting non-essential cookies before consent is given
  • make it as easy to refuse as to accept
  • keep cookie information clear and up to date

6. How users can manage cookies

Most browsers allow users to block or delete cookies through browser settings. However, blocking some cookies may affect website functionality.

7. Third-party cookies

If we add third-party services in future, those providers may set cookies on our website. This could include analytics providers, embedded video or social media tools, advertising platforms, and email marketing tools. We will update this Cookie Policy as our website develops.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the cookies we use, the website, or legal requirements. The latest version will always appear on this page with the updated date shown above.
